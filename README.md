# The One-Eyed Pirate Shark & the Dancing Octopus

A co-op underwater treasure hunt for little kids. No game over.

- **Play (tablet/phone, octopus buddy):** `index-v2.html`
- **Play (two players):** `index.html`
- On iPhone: open in Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen** to play full screen like an app.


# Reading Games

Sight-word and number games (Word Monsters, Shark Snack Attack, Shark Sentences, Shark Numbers) in `reading/`.
- Play: `reading/index.html`
- On iPhone: open in Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**.

## Shark Snack Attack 3D
`reading/shark-3d.html`. To change its words, edit `reading/words.js` (one word per quote, comma between words).

## Shark Island After Dark
`reading/shark-island.html`. One game that changes every night: drive the monster truck (numbers), a surprise challenge (map, +1 or counting by 2s and 5s), then dive in the submarine and finish the word to rescue a shark.
- Hold **Grown-ups** on the home screen to set how scary it is, the ranger and truck names, the word list, and to see what he misses most.
- New sharks, truck parts and words are lists at the top of the file (`SPECIES`, `PARTS`, `START_WORDS`).
